The Heart Behind Dollhouse

Meet Sharletha Barrett

Sharletha isn't just a stylist — she's the vision behind The Dollhouse Salon, a space born from the belief that hair is a crown. After years of mastering silk presses and custom colors, she opened her doors in Lakewood to create something beyond ordinary.

She specializes in silk presses that bring hair back to life and custom colors that turn heads — always with healthy hair at the forefront. Her philosophy? Your hair, your crown. Every appointment is a royal experience: warm, personalized, and designed to make you feel powerful the moment you walk out.

"I opened Dollhouse to build a salon where women don't just leave looking beautiful — they leave feeling unstoppable."
